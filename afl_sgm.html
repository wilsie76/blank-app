3. Help

Banker = max probability the SGM cashes. Picks the safest legs that still give a payout above your floor.

Value = max expected value. Hunts genuine model-vs-market edges; higher variance.

Model % blends form-based Gaussian on last-5 with the de-vigged market price.

Est book $ approximates Sportsbet's SGM quote after correlation discount. Compare to the actual quote before betting.
